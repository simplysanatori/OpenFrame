# Controls

The Frame's controllers are the two halves of an Xbox controller. The right one holds and aims the weapon. If you play left-handed, the two swap: the sticks, the pouch and the holsters mirror, and "the turning stick" below means your left stick.

## The controllers

|---|---|

| Head | Look. Crouch in the room and the game crouches |
| Right trigger | Fire |
| Left trigger | Second weapon in a Banshee. On foot you throw grenades by hand |
| Left stick | Move |
| Left stick click | Crouch, at any speed. While sprinting, slide (PARKOUR on) |
| Turning stick, left and right | Turn, in steps or smoothly |
| Turning stick, flick up | Sprint on or off (SPRINT set to TOGGLE) |
| Turning stick, flick down | Bullet time on or off (BULLET TIME on) |
| Turning stick, held down 0.8 seconds | Third person on or off. Needs no setting |
| Turning stick, pressed in | Zoom, as in the flat game, in bullet time as well |
| **A** | Jump |
| **B** | Melee. A hard swing of the right controller melees too |
| **X** | Action and reload. Hold to pick up a weapon |
| **Y** | Switch weapon |
| Left bumper | Flashlight |
| Right bumper | Switch grenade type |
| D-pad | Menus |
| **View** | Back, and the multiplayer scoreboard |
| **Menu** | Pause |
| Both grips, held | Recentre |
| Right grip | Draw a weapon from a holster, over the shoulder or at the hip |

## The turning stick's gestures

- A flick counts when the stick goes within about 34 degrees of straight up or down. While the stick is inside that cone, turning pauses, so a flick never turns you. A diagonal push only turns you.
- Flick up: sprint on or off, at the moment of the push.
- Flick down and let go within 0.8 seconds: bullet time on or off. It switches as you let go, and rests half a second between switches.
- Hold down for 0.8 seconds: third person on or off.
- Pressed in: zoom. Nothing holds the click.
- There is no magazine eject on the stick. Reload with **X**, or take a magazine from your left hip.
- Sprint, bullet time and the third person show a label on the HUD when they switch.

The GESTURES page, under MOVEMENT AND COMFORT, offers other bindings for bullet time: a held click (a short press still zooms), a plain click, a double flick down, a double flick up, a held push up, or off. The third person can be a double flick down instead of a hold.

## The added features

These come from moistman42069's Halo VR mod, rewritten for OpenH1's renderer and the Frame's controllers. Sprint, bullet time, parkour, fists, weapon pull, prop throw and hand swap work in the campaign and are each off until you turn them on in VR Setup. The third person works in the campaign with no setting. The wrist HUD and the vignette are options for everywhere.

### MOVEMENT AND COMFORT

- **SPRINT**: TOGGLE for the stick flick, or ARM SWING to run faster by swinging both arms. **SPRINT SPEED** sets how much faster, 1.5 times by default. Crouching pauses a sprint.
- **BULLET TIME**: the world slows to 40 % while your head and hands stay at full speed. Your own body slows with the world, so jumps float. **BULLET TIME SPEED** sets the slowdown. It ends by itself at a cinematic or when you die.
- **THIRD PERSON** and **VIEW DISTANCE**: the camera floats 1.7 m behind you, over the shoulder of your weapon hand, and tucks in at walls. You still aim with the controller. Zooming, a vehicle or a cinematic puts you back in first person. Not in multiplayer.
- **PARKOUR**: slide, wall jump and wall run.
  - Slide: crouch while sprinting at full speed, by the stick click or by ducking your head. A click-started slide runs its whole way with the stick let go, up to 1.1 seconds. Click again to stand early. **SLIDE MOMENTUM** sets its length and speed.
  - Wall jump: in the air beside a wall, press **A** again. You leap where you look. **WALL JUMP** sets the strength.
  - Wall run: jump at a wall with the left stick pushed along it. You run along the wall for up to two seconds. Steer away to drop, or jump off it.
- **VIGNETTE**: VEHICLES as in OpenH1, ALWAYS to also narrow the view when you move or turn on foot, or OFF. **VIGNETTE STRENGTH** sets how much.

### HANDS AND WEAPONS, under FISTS AND GRABBING

- **FISTS**: your free hand punches. Swing it at an enemy at 2 m/s or more. A hard swing staggers them for a second. Swipe across an enemy's melee as it lands to parry it; the HUD says "Parry". **PUNCH SPEED** and **PUNCH DAMAGE** tune it.
- **WEAPON PULL**: hold your free hand out, point it at a weapon on the floor within **PULL RANGE**, 3 m by default, squeeze the grip and flick the hand back. The weapon flies to your hand. Keep the grip squeezed to catch it.
- **PROP THROW**: squeeze the grip with your free hand on a loose weapon or piece of equipment to pick it up, and let go to throw it. **THROW STRENGTH** scales the throw.
- **HAND SWAP**: bring your empty hand to the weapon, squeeze its grip and open the other. The weapon changes hands, and the game remembers which hand is yours. The sticks stay where they were.

### HUD

- **HUD STYLE**: VISOR as in OpenH1, or WRIST to carry shields, ammo and the motion tracker on the back of your off hand's wrist. Turn the wrist toward your face to read it. **WRIST HUD** sets its size and place on the arm. The reticle, messages and waypoints stay on the visor, and the visor comes back in menus and in vehicles.
- **HUD LAYOUT**: sliders in percent to bring the corners in, and to move or resize the ammo, shields and tracker, together or one by one. Changes apply when you press OK on the page.
